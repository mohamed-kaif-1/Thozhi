# 05 — User Research Interview Guide & Protocol

## 1. Research Protocol Overview
- **Project:** THOZHI
- **Stage:** 0.1 — Problem Validation Preparation
- **Target Segment:** Bilingual Freelancers & Solo Consultants (with secondary knowledge workers)
- **Target Sample Size:** 6 to 8 exploratory participants
- **Interview Duration:** 10 to 15 minutes per session
- **Execution Status:** **PENDING HUMAN EXECUTION (Awaiting Project Owner Field Action)**
- **Methodological Standard:** "The Mom Test" (Rob Fitzpatrick) — Investigate actual historical behavior, never ask hypothetical opinions or pitch the proposed solution.

---

## 2. Participant Recruitment Screener & Criteria

### 2.1 Mandatory Inclusion Criteria
1. **Role:** Active freelancer, independent contractor, solo consultant, or agency owner managing $\ge 3$ distinct client projects or work streams simultaneously.
2. **Linguistic Context:** Regularly communicates, thinks, or notes thoughts in a mix of Tamil and English (Tanglish) or regional bilingual vernacular.
3. **Planning Friction:** Self-identifies as experiencing periodic task overload, missed deadlines, or scheduling conflict stress.
4. **Tool Usage:** Actively uses at least one digital tool (WhatsApp self-chat, Apple Notes, Google Calendar, Todoist, Notion, Trello) or physical notebook for task tracking.

### 2.2 Disqualification Criteria
- Full-time corporate employees whose entire schedule is rigidly dictated by external management without personal autonomy.
- Individuals who speak exclusively English or exclusively formal Tamil without code-mixing in daily life.
- Professional project managers who have already perfected a zero-friction personal workflow and report zero planning anxiety.

## 3. Behavioral Interview Script (10–15 Minutes)

> **Interviewer Mindset Directive:**  
> - **DO NOT PITCH THOZHI.** Do not mention "AI planning assistant," "Tamil voice app," or "smart scheduling."  
> - If the participant asks what you are building, reply: *"We are studying how independent professionals manage daily responsibilities and where current workflows break down."*  
> - Dig into specific past instances: *"When was the last time that happened?"*, *"What did you do next?"*, *"Can you show me?"*

### 3.1 Warm-up & Context (2 Minutes)
1. "Could you briefly describe what kind of freelance or consulting projects you are currently juggling?"
2. "Roughly how many different clients or external stakeholders are you dealing with this month?"

### 3.2 Past Behavioral Capture & Processing (5 Minutes)
3. "Think back to the last time you felt completely overwhelmed with too many tasks and deadlines. Walk me through that specific day."
   - *Probe:* How did you capture everything you had to do? (Voice, typing, paper, WhatsApp?)
   - *Probe:* Did you write down everything in English, Tamil, or a mix of both?
4. "How did you decide what to work on first that morning? What was your actual thought process?"
5. "Can you open your phone or notebook right now and show me the last 3 things you wrote down or recorded to remember?"
   - *Analysis Check:* Are they discrete commitments, vague thoughts, or conditional ideas?

### 3.3 Conflict Management & Failure Modes (5 Minutes)
6. "Tell me about a specific time recently when two important deadlines or commitments collided on the same day. What happened?"
   - *Probe:* When did you realize they were conflicting—days in advance or on the day itself?
   - *Probe:* What did you end up delaying, dropping, or staying up late to finish?
7. "What tools (apps, calendars, notebooks) did you use to manage that situation?"
8. "What specifically annoyed or frustrated you about those tools when things got chaotic?"
9. "When an unexpected client emergency or family request arrives at 2:00 PM, what happens to the rest of your planned day?"

### 3.4 Language & Spoken Capture Patterns (3 Minutes)
10. "Do you ever record voice notes to yourself or send voice notes to colleagues to explain tasks? Why or why not?"
    - *Probe:* If yes, what language do you speak in? Do you mix Tamil and English?
    - *Probe:* What is annoying about going back to listen to or transcribe those voice notes?
11. "Have you spent money on any productivity, calendar, or task management software in the past 12 months? Which ones, and why?"

## 4. Participant Consent & Privacy Protocol

1. **Informed Consent:**  
   Prior to starting, read: *"Thank you for participating. This is an independent research interview to understand daily planning workflows. We will not sell your data or quote your name publicly. May we have your permission to take notes and record audio purely for internal transcription?"*
2. **Audio & Data Handling:**  
   - Audio recordings must be stored in encrypted local storage, never uploaded to public clouds.
   - PII (names, client names, proprietary business data) mentioned during interviews must be anonymized in the research notes.
3. **Right to Withdraw:**  
   Participants may halt the interview or request deletion of their data at any point.

## 6. Evidence Classification Framework

Each completed interview must be categorized according to strict empirical criteria:

| Category | Empirical Indicator | Impact on THOZHI Validation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Strong Positive** | Participant experiences acute weekly planning conflict; currently uses messy WhatsApp/paper workarounds; captures in Tanglish; has paid for workflow software; lost revenue or high stress due to dropped commitments. | Validates Problem Hypothesis & Opportunities A, B, C, D. |
| **Weak Positive** | Participant admits to occasional disorganization but copes passably with Google Calendar or memory; expresses mild interest but has never paid for productivity tools. | Problem exists but lacks commercial urgency; suggests freemium or consumer friction. |
| **Neutral** | Uses standard English exclusively; has a structured corporate routine; unaffected by multilingual friction. | Disconfirms Opportunity D for this user persona. |
| **Disconfirming (Falsification)** | Participant explicitly prefers open-ended paper notes; rejects structured time-blocking as stifling; finds typing in English effortless; refuses voice capture due to privacy or open office environment. | Contradicts Core Product Assumptions (A-BEH-01, A-BEH-02). |
